# Mantle identity brief

## Essence

Mantle is the dependable layer between a person and the tools they use every
day. It makes a development environment portable, understandable, and owned by
the user without taking over their shell or hiding consequential behavior.

## Visual direction

Express a quiet technical system made from explicit, composable layers. The
mark combines a shell prompt with nested protective strata: cyan represents
clear intent, violet represents composition, and rose represents the human at
the center of the tooling. Use deep ink surfaces, crisp geometry, generous
space, and high-contrast type. The result should feel calm and capable rather
than flashy or militarized.

## Voice

Lead with the exact operation, scope, and current state. Explain consequences
before asking for confirmation. Prefer plain, direct language; name uncertainty
honestly; and always pair a failure with a recovery path.

## Avoid

- flames, armor, command-and-control imagery, or destructive power symbolism;
- neon cyberpunk noise, fake terminal text, or decorative code fragments;
- language that implies Mantle owns a user's machine, dotfiles, or decisions;
- success claims when a result is partial, unverified, or awaiting review.
